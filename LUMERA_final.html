DIRECT CONCIERGE INQUIRIES →
What skin types is Luméra suitable for?
+
Luméra Barrier Repair Serum is formulated to be suitable for most skin types, including sensitive, dry, combination, and normal skin. The lightweight formula with ceramides, hyaluronic acid, and centella extract makes it a gentle yet effective option for daily use across different skin conditions.
How often should I use it?
+
Luméra can be used twice daily—morning and evening—as part of your skincare routine. Apply a few drops after cleansing and before your moisturizer. Many users find that even using it once daily delivers noticeable results, so you can adjust frequency based on your skin's needs.
When should I apply the serum?
+
Apply Luméra after cleansing but before moisturizing. This allows the serum to penetrate properly and create a foundation for your moisturizer. Pat your skin gently after cleansing (leaving it slightly damp helps with absorption) and apply a few drops of the serum, then follow with your regular moisturizer.
Does it feel sticky?
+
No, Luméra has a lightweight, silky texture that absorbs quickly without leaving a sticky residue. The formula is specifically designed to feel comfortable on the skin, making it ideal for layering under moisturizers or makeup without any occlusive drag or pilling.
How much product is in the bottle?
+
Each bottle contains 30 ml (1.0 fl. oz.) of serum. This size is carefully calculated to provide approximately 2-3 months of daily use when applying a few drops per application, making it both economical and convenient.
Can I use it with my existing skincare routine?
+
Yes, Luméra is designed to integrate seamlessly into most skincare routines. Apply it after cleansing and before your regular moisturizer. It works well with other products and won't interfere with treatments like retinoids or vitamin C serums, though we recommend patch testing if you have very sensitive skin.
